About The Artist

Nicole loved reading and drawing as a child, and was drawn to comic books very early on. Inspired by both Western and Eastern style comics, they have incorporated both in their artwork.

Nicole currently resides in their hometown of Belleville, Illinois and continues growing their passion for art!
